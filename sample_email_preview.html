Journal of feline medicine and surgery · 2026-10-21
Summary: This study analyzed phosphorus and calcium levels in various cat purée treats and found that mineral content varies significantly, which is important for cats managing chronic kidney disease.
摘要：研究分析了多种猫用肉泥零食，发现其中的磷和钙含量差异巨大，这对患有慢性肾病的猫非常重要。
